# The Utilitarian Failure of Mandatory Real-Name Systems

A critical thinking reflection essay and supporting Chinese oral presentation script evaluating **mandatory Internet Real-Name Systems (RNS)** through Utilitarian ethics, empirical evidence from South Korea's 2007–2012 policy experiment, and an **"Accountable Anonymity"** alternative framework.

## Contents
- `essay_utilitarian_failure_rns.md` / `.docx` / `.pdf` — Analytical reflection essay (494 words) evaluating mandatory RNS via Mill's Utilitarianism, the 2011 *Nate* data breach (35 million users), empirical ineffectiveness (0.9% reduction in malicious comments vs. significant chilling effect), and the 2012 Korean Constitutional Court ruling
- `speech_script_zh.md` / `.docx` — Chinese oral presentation script contrasting pro-order, pro-rights, and least-restrictive neutral perspectives, and proposing *Accountable Anonymity* (intermediary liability, strong data privacy laws, and warrant-based judicial/technical tracing)

## Context & Attribution
- **Group Presentation Context**: Produced alongside a group critical thinking presentation on online privacy. The shared group slide deck is intentionally excluded from this repository to protect teammates' names and student IDs.
